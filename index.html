What we do

End-to-end permit management

From first submission to final approval — we own the entire process on your behalf.

01

Wayleave Applications

Full preparation and submission of wayleave applications to SANRAL, Eskom, Transnet, and all municipal road authorities across South Africa.

  • Document compilation & engineering drawing review
  • Authority liaison and follow-up
  • Approval tracking and reporting
02

Road & Traffic Permits

Abnormal load permits, road closure authorisations, and traffic accommodation plans coordinated with relevant road authorities.

03

Municipal Authorisations

Rezoning, building plan approvals, land-use applications, and bylaws compliance — we know what each municipality requires.

04

Utility Coordination

Eskom, water boards, Telkom and regional utility coordination for crossing approvals and service relocations.

05

Permit Compliance Audits

Review of existing permits for compliance gaps, expiry tracking, and renewal management before project sign-off.

06

Retainer & Project Support

Monthly retainer arrangements for contractors and developers with ongoing permitting needs — priority turnaround guaranteed.

How it works

From enquiry to approval

01

Enquiry & Scoping

Tell us about your project. We assess what permits are required, timelines, and provide a fee proposal — usually within 24 hours.

02

Document Preparation

We compile your full application package — drawings, motivation letters, site coordinates, and any authority-specific requirements.

03

Submission & Liaison

We submit directly to the relevant authorities and maintain ongoing communication until a decision is made.

04

Approval & Handover

Once approved, you receive your permit documentation with a full compliance summary. Your project can proceed.
