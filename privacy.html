1. Who We Are

PermitFlow SA (Pty) Ltd (Reg No: 2026/352999/07) is the responsible party (as defined under POPIA) for personal information collected through this website and in the course of providing our services.

PermitFlow SA is a private B2B consulting firm. We are not a government entity, municipality, or state department. We do not represent or act on behalf of any public authority in the collection of personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact details: Full name, email address, phone number, company name
  • Project information: Project location, permit type, project timeline, and related technical details you share with us
  • Business information: Company registration details, VAT numbers (where required for invoicing)
  • Communication records: Emails, enquiry form submissions, and other correspondence
  • Website usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and session duration (collected via cookies and analytics tools)

We do not collect sensitive personal information (as defined under POPIA) unless absolutely necessary and with your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing quotes for our consulting services
  • Preparing and submitting permit applications on your behalf (as per your service agreement)
  • Communicating project status updates and requesting required documentation
  • Issuing invoices and processing payments
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improving our website and services through anonymised analytics
  • Sending occasional relevant service updates (you may opt out at any time)

We will not use your personal information for any purpose incompatible with the above without your prior consent.

4. Sharing of Information

We share your personal information only where necessary:

  • Government authorities and municipalities: Your project details and contact information are shared with the relevant authority as part of the permit application process — this is the core purpose of our service and you consent to this when engaging us
  • Service providers: We use third-party tools such as Formspree (contact forms) and Airtable (project management) — these providers are bound by their own privacy policies and GDPR/POPIA-compliant data processing terms
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or regulatory authority

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, and in accordance with applicable legal requirements:

  • Client records and project documentation: 5 years from project completion (in line with standard South African business record-keeping requirements)
  • Enquiry and contact form data: 12 months if no engagement results
  • Financial records: 5 years as required by South African tax law
  • Website analytics: Anonymised, retained for up to 26 months

After the applicable retention period, personal information is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS encryption on our website
  • Access controls limiting who within our team can view client data
  • Use of reputable, security-certified third-party platforms for data storage

While we take security seriously, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required under POPIA.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information and obtain a copy
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate or incomplete personal information be corrected
  • Deletion: Request the deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent at any time
  • Complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zwelethu@permitflow.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and gather anonymised usage data. Types of cookies we may use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymised data to help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g. Google Analytics)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality but will not prevent you from accessing our content.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website and operations make use of the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Formspree — contact form submission processing (formspree.io)
  • Airtable — project management and client tracking (airtable.com)
  • Google Fonts — typography rendering (fonts.googleapis.com)
  • Cloudflare Pages — website hosting (cloudflare.com)

Each of these providers maintains their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We only use services that offer adequate data protection standards.
